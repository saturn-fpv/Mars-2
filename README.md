# Mars 2"

### 2" Unibody FPV Frame

The **Mars 2** is a lightweight and durable **90 mm wheelbase unibody frame** designed for high-performance 2-inch FPV builds. It provides excellent rigidity and smooth flight characteristics. This is the perfect park ripper.

## ✨ Features
- **Unibody 2" frame** for maximum rigidity and reduced vibrations  
- **Optimized geometry** with truss-style reinforced arms
- **Central mounting platform** for clean AIO or stack installation
- **Octa-shape base** rigid and enough space for 90° USB-C or Micro-USB ports
- **90 mm wheelbase** for agile indoor/outdoor flying  
- **Wide motor protection zones** for better crash resistance  
- **Compatible with analog and HD systems** (DJI O4, HDZero, Walksnail, analog)  
- **Lightweight** for improved efficiency and flight time
- **Step file** for your customization needs
- **Flywoo battery holder** version for ease of use, as well as without

---

## 🔧 Build Specifications & Recommendations

### **Frame Thickness**
- **2 mm carbon fiber:** Balanced between strength and weight, ideal for high-KV micro motors.

### **Motor Recommendations**
- **1002, 1003, 1102, 1103 motors** with a **6.6 mm bolt circle** (3.3 mm hole radius from center), 3 × M1.4
- Kv range:  
  - **2S builds:** 9000–14000 Kv  
  - **3S builds:** 6000–9000 Kv  

### **Propeller Size**
- **2" (51 mm) propellers**  
  Recommended options:
  - Gemfan 2009 tri-blade (reference build)
  - Gemfan 2015 bi-blade
  - Gemfan 2023S tri-blade
  - HQProp 2X1X3 tri-blade
  - HQProp 2X1.5 bi-blade

### **Screw Sizes**
- **Motor screws:** M1.6 × 4–5 mm (depending on motor base)  
- **AIO / stack mounting:**  
  - 25.5×25.5 AIO → M2 × 10-18mm (depending on stack and canopy) 

### **3D Print Recommendations**
Material: **TPU (95A)**  
Suggested prints:
- Canopy (any 25.5mm whoop canopy)
- Battery holder (optional)

---

## 🏗️ Build Tips
- Tighten the stack screw with a nut under the AIO to reduce noise  
- Keep battery centered for better CG  
- For DJI O4 builds: tug away the cables neatly
- Keep wiring short for better performance and reliability
